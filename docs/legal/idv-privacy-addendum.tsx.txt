      <Section id="idv" heading="5. Identity verification and biometric data">
        <p>
          To meet our legal and fraud-prevention obligations, we ask some users
          to verify their identity. The check is carried out by{" "}
          <strong>Veriff OÜ</strong>, an identity verification provider
          established in Estonia, acting as our processor.
        </p>
        <p>During a verification, Veriff collects and processes:</p>
        <List
          items={[
            "Images or video of your identity document (passport, national ID, driving licence or residence permit), and the data printed or encoded on it — including document number, date of birth, nationality and expiry date.",
            "An image or video of your face, and the biometric measurements derived from it in order to confirm that you are a real, live person and that you match the document.",
            "Where you use the upload option instead of the guided flow, the files you upload.",
            "Technical signals about the device and network used, for fraud detection.",
          ]}
        />
        <p>
          Facial images processed for the purpose of uniquely identifying you
          are <strong>biometric data</strong>. Under the DPA this is sensitive
          personal data, and under GDPR Article 9 it is a special category of
          personal data. We rely on your{" "}
          <strong>explicit consent</strong> to process it. You give that consent
          before the check begins, and we record when you gave it. You can
          refuse — but we may then be unable to offer you features that depend
          on a verified identity.
        </p>

        <Sub heading="Automated decision-making" />
        <p>
          Veriff&apos;s decision is produced substantially by automated means,
          and it can affect whether you may use parts of Canja. You have the
          right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated
          processing where it produces legal or similarly significant effects.
          If you are declined, you may contact us at{" "}
          support@syncra.co.ke to obtain human review, to
          express your point of view, and to contest the outcome.
        </p>

        <Sub heading="What we keep" />
        <p>
          We store only the outcome of the check — whether it passed, when it
          happened, and Veriff&apos;s session reference. We do not store your
          document images or your face image on our own systems. We keep that
          outcome for seven years after your account closes, because it
          evidences the identity checks we are required to perform.
        </p>
        <p>
          Veriff holds the images, video and extracted document data under its
          own retention schedule and its contract with us. That period is
          currently 12 months from the date of the verification, after which
          Veriff deletes them.
        </p>
      </Section>
